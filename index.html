Why it stalls

Recording it was the easy part.

You serve clients all week and make content with whatever time is left. So the videos sit in a folder.

Getting it transcribed, writing the post, cutting the clip, finding it again later. Each one is its own job, and together they wait for a free afternoon that never comes.

EchoOS puts all of it in one app. You stay the source and the last edit.

You're not behind

You told me you want to get more out of the videos you already make, and right now you're sitting on videos you never used.

That's common when you serve clients and make your own content. You're doing two jobs, and the second one gets whatever time is left.

The bad news

The slow part starts after you hit stop. Transcribe it, write the post, cut the clip, file it. Each step waits for a free afternoon, and in a client week the video stays in the folder.

The good news

EchoOS keeps that whole path in one app. Paste a link or drop in a file, and you get a timestamped transcript. Ask for a post and it drafts one in your voice. Ask for clips, pick one of up to five, and it's cut to an MP4, vertical included.

The bad news

AI writing from a blank prompt has nothing of yours to go on, so it reaches for the most common way to say it. Editing that back into your voice can take as long as writing it yourself.

The good news

EchoOS starts from you. You build a voice profile in setup, and drafts start from the transcript of what you said on camera. It stays a draft until you say it's done. You're the last edit.

The bad news

I build around one rule: each piece of content has one job, to start a conversation with the right person. A post written for everyone gets a like and a scroll. And when one post takes hours, there's no time left to decide its job.

The good news

EchoOS can't pick the job for you, and no tool can promise you clients. It takes the slow part off your plate, so your time goes to what the post says and who it's for.

The bad news

A routine with a handful of manual steps breaks the first busy week. Every step that needs you is a place it stops, and starting again feels like starting from zero.

The good news

EchoOS cuts the steps down. Add a folder and new videos show up on their own, transcribed if you want. Search your transcripts by what was said. Ask for clips or a post in one chat. The post comes back as a draft you can revise.
