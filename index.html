Why it stalls

Recording it was the easy part.

You serve clients all week. So the videos sit in a folder.

Transcribe it, write the post, cut the clip, find it later. None of it fits in a client week.

EchoOS puts all of it in one app. You stay the source and the last edit.

How it works

Your first video in three steps.

  1. Set up once

    Connect your own AI and set up your voice. Skip either and come back later.

  2. Bring a video

    Paste a YouTube, TikTok or Instagram link, or drop in a file.

  3. Ask, then approve

    Ask for posts and clips in one chat. Approve what you'll use.
